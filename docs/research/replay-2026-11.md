# Replay of the index scalps, 2026-10-08 to 2026-11-04

Written 2026-11-04, after two live sessions of the Index 0DTE Trader placed one order in total. Tool: `tools/replay/` (see the strategy-replay skill). Every number here is reproducible with the commands below, on the same bars.

**Not financial advice.** These are modeled results on 20 sessions. They compare rule sets with each other. They do not predict what a live session earns. SPY and QQQ are the instruments the user's `.env` allows; they are not recommendations.

## Setup

- Bars: broker 1-minute and 5-minute regular-session bars for SPY and QQQ, 2026-10-07 to 2026-11-04 (21 sessions; the first warms up indicators and gives the prior-day levels, so 20 are traded).
- Indicator check: EMA(9), EMA(20), VWAP, and ATR(14) on the replay's 5-minute bars match the values the agent journaled from the broker on 2026-11-04 to the cent.
- Options: same-day contracts priced with Black-Scholes. Implied volatility calibrated on four journaled quotes: SPY 8.8% (two quotes), QQQ 11.7% and 16.0%, on a trading-minute year. Defaults SPY 9%, QQQ 13%. Model deltas 0.39 to 0.45 against broker deltas 0.38 to 0.42.
- Orders: per-order cap $250. Strike walk from the first out-of-the-money strike, delta floor 0.35. Entry one minute after the signal bar closes. Cost 0.03 per contract per round trip. One position at a time.
- Exits ("ATR bracket"): bank order at `entry + delta × 0.8 × ATR(14, 5m)`, premium stop at the tighter of `entry − delta × 0.8 × ATR` and 25% below entry, strategy stop on a 1-minute close, time stop 30 minutes, inside a 1-minute bar the stop is tested first.

### Bracket: percent of premium against ATR-scaled

Both strategies, PF by implied volatility level:

| IV (SPY, QQQ) | Bank +30%, stop −25% | ATR bracket |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7%, 11% | 1.18 | 1.25 |
| 9%, 13% | 1.39 | 1.42 |
| 11%, 15% | 0.81 | 1.20 |
| 13%, 18% | 0.70 | 1.03 |

A fixed percent needs a larger move in the underlying when premium is expensive, and the setups do not deliver it in time. The ATR bracket sizes the bank price to the move the setup predicts.

### The old trend-scalp stop

With the stop at the pullback's lowest low, setups A and B allowed 7 trades in 20 sessions (all day). With the stop at the signal bar's extreme (the change made live on 2026-11-04), 55. The live agents skipped six signals on 2026-11-04 for the old stop distance alone.

### 2026-11-04 itself

Live: no orders. Replay of both strategies: 9 trades, net +39. Replay of the first draft, with a +30% / −25% bracket: −58. It was a two-way day, and most stops fired within 1 to 9 minutes.

## What changed in the repo

- New strategy index-level-scalp and the key-levels skill.
- index-trend-scalp: entry cutoff 11:30 and bracket exits for options. Its late-session rules are gone.
- trade-management: the bracket.
- position-sizing: the strike walk, shared by every option strategy.
- Setting `DEEPTRADE_DAILY_PROFIT_TARGET_PCT`, default 0 (off).
- index-intraday-momentum is no longer preloaded by the Index 0DTE Trader. On gap days its target sits behind the price.

## Limits

- 20 sessions, one regime (a rising market into a midterm election). Re-run monthly.
- The rules were written after looking at these sessions. The 11:30 cutoff in particular was chosen from them. A forward test on new sessions is the real check.
- Constant implied volatility, no spread widening, modeled fills.
- The replay takes the first symbol with a signal (SPY before QQQ). Live, index-pair-read picks.

## Reproduce

```text
node tools/replay/bars.js journal/replay <saved get_equity_historicals responses>
node tools/replay/run.js --strategy index-level-scalp,index-trend-scalp --hours
node tools/replay/run.js --strategy index-level-scalp,index-trend-scalp --to 15:25 --hours
node tools/replay/run.js --strategy random
node tools/replay/run.js --strategy index-level-scalp,index-trend-scalp --bank 1.3 --stop-atr 0 --iv SPY=0.11,QQQ=0.15
```
